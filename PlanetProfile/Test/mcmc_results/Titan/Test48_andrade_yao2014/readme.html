Titan — Test 48: Andrade + Yao 2014 spherical convection

Body template: PPTest48.py. Driver: Test48_mcmc_andrade_yao2014.py.

Difference from Test 46

  1. Structure cache rebuilt with Yao et al. (2014) spherical-shell convection scaling for Ice Ih instead of Deschamps & Sotin (2001). Produces thicker stagnant lids and lower basal heat flux for Titan's geometry (f ≈ 0.96).
  2. Adds heat-flux consistency constraint: sampled Ih viscosity must produce physically plausible convective heat flux via Yao scaling.
    • Low ηIh → high Ra → high q (penalized if q ≫ qobs)
    • High ηIh → Ra < Racrit → conductive (penalized)
