Titan — Test 46: Andrade hybrid hydrosphere

Body template: PPTest46.py. Driver: Test46_mcmc_andrade_hybrid_hydro.py.

Body / template

  • Titan analog. R = 2575.5 km, ρ = 1.880 ± 0.004 g/cm³.
  • Hybrid hydrosphere: Dhydro is a free parameter; PP CMR² closure relaxed (CMR² becomes a model output).
  • Cores: rho_core ≥ rho_sil constraint (stable stratification).

Model assumptions

  • Andrade rheology with HP ices sharing one viscosity.
  • Hydrosphere PT self-consistent (driven by Tb); silicate top at R − Dhydro.
  • Tb × Dhydro grid: Tb = 252–253 K at 0.02 K (fine, 50 pts) + 253–270 K at 0.1 K (171 pts) = 221 Tb pts; D = 50–800 km at 25 km (31 pts). Total ≈ 6851 grid points.
