Titan — Test 45: Maxwell hybrid hydrosphere

Body template: PPTest45.py. Driver: Test45_mcmc_maxwell_hybrid_hydro.py.

Body / template

  • Titan analog. R = 2575.5 km, ρ = 1.880 ± 0.004 g/cm³ → M ≈ 1.34534 × 1023 kg.
  • Total hydrosphere thickness Dhydro treated as free parameter; PP CMR² closure relaxed.

Model assumptions

  • Maxwell rheology applied to ices and silicate.
  • Hydrosphere PT remains self-consistent (driven by Tb); silicate top placed at R − Dhydro.
  • Mtot and CMR² are model outputs (not enforced); compared to observations in likelihood.
  • Pre-computed Tb × Dhydro grid (Tb = 252–270 K at 0.1 K, D = 50–800 km at 50 km) → ~2896 grid points.
