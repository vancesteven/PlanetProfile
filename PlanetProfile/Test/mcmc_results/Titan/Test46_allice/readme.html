Titan — Test 46: All-ice (no ocean), Andrade rheology

Body template: PPTest46_allice.py. Driver: Test46_mcmc_allice.py.

Hypothesis

Tests Petricca et al. (2025) all-ice scenario: NO liquid ocean; the entire hydrosphere is ice (Ih + III + V + VI). With NO_OCEAN_EXCEPT_INNER_ICES=True and Fe_CORE=False, ice structure is essentially insensitive to Tb (< 1 km variation across 240–251 K). The all-ice hypothesis produces a unique structure.

Body / template

  • Titan analog. R = 2574.73 km, M = 1.3452 × 1023 kg.
  • Fixed structure at Tb = 250 K: DiceIh ≈ 156 km, DiceIII ≈ 83 km, DiceV ≈ 155 km, DiceVI ≈ 69 km, Dtot ≈ 494 km, ρsil ≈ 2555 kg/m³, CMR² ≈ 0.343.

Model assumptions

  • Andrade rheology applied to all ices and silicate; Fe = elastic.
  • Per-phase HP ice viscosities sampled independently (each phase has distinct shear modulus and dissipation).
  • MCMC samples rheology only at fixed structure (7D).
  • Two variants present: legacy (Deschamps & Sotin 2001 convection) and Yao 2014 spherical convection.
